3-Min Grounding

Micro-Practice You Can Do Anywhere

  1. Pause & Posture - Sit tall, soften your shoulders, gently close your eyes.
  2. 4-4-6 Breath - Inhale through the nose for 4, hold for 4, exhale slowly for 6.
  3. Feel & Release - Notice where the body is holding tension and let it melt with each exhale.

Repeat this cycle for 3-5 minutes to reset your mental state before work, study, or sleep.

Inside a MindfulIntentions Meditation Session

  1. Arrival & Grounding - Slow breathing, posture check, and a gentle body scan to bring you into the present moment.
  2. Breathwork Layer - Simple patterns like box breathing, 4-4-6, or humming exhale to calm the nervous system.
  3. Guided Visualization - Instructor-led inner journeys focused on safety, clarity, and emotional release.
  4. Silent Integration - A short pocket of silence where you simply sit with your own breath and awareness.
  5. Closing Intention - Soft prompts to anchor what you felt into a clear, practical intention for your day or night.
